## Verdict

**Not ready to execute.** The evidence base is strong: every one of the 52 spot-checked numbers reproduces, including 10 oracle re-runs, and the rot register misses only three map 12 items.

The problem is that PLAN and VERIFICATION were written separately and contradict each other on the material agents touch first: gate IDs, xtask gates, fixture paths, fixture kinds and the committed fluid set (C01-C04). They also disagree on core policies: what `Investigate` asserts, the `libm` rule, the cubic-R entry, and the FP and `set_config_*` behaviour of the C shim (C05-C09). And PLAN carries neither the rot register's IDs nor its GAP mechanisms (C10, C11).

Fix C01-C09 and C13 before M0 starts. C10-C12 and C14-C16 must be fixed before the milestones they touch. The minors can be fixed in passing.
