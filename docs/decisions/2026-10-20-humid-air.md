# Humid air (2026-10-20)

Asked when the user said to start on humid air. M18, humid air, had a roadmap row and no steps; its entry is "M14
done (ice)", and M14, IF97's five regions with their backward equations and ice, had not started. RP-1485, the model
v8.0.0's `HAPropsSI` implements (map 07 §3.3), takes water's saturation pressure and the compressibility of liquid water
from IAPWS-IF97 at 0 °C and above, and the sublimation pressure (IAPWS R14-08) and ice (IAPWS R10-06) below (ASHRAE's
LibHuAirProp user guide, §1.3, which implements RP-1485); IF97's and IAPWS-95's saturation pressures differ by 6e-5 at
300 K (map 07 H3). The full RP-1485 report, with its equations and the tables of virial coefficients, enhancement
factor, k_T and Henry's constant that v8.0.0's tests copy, is sold by ASHRAE. Free: the authors' 14 tables of moist-air
properties (Herrmann, Kretzschmar and Gatley, a supplement to HVAC&R Research 15(5), 2009, on their faculty's site), and
IAPWS R7-97(2012), R10-06(2009), R14-08(2011), G7-04, G8-10 and G11-15. Tsilingiris (2008), the mixing rule of
`HAPropsSI`'s viscosity and conductivity, is paywalled.
