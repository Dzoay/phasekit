fastchebpure data (LicenseRef-fastchebpure)
===========================================

The files under crates/phasekit-verify/fixtures/mp/fastchebpure-*/ are converted
from the multiprecision saturation check files (outputcheck/<Fluid>_check.json)
of NIST's fastchebpure, release 2026.06.02-v2
(https://github.com/usnistgov/fastchebpure; the archive pinned by
crates/phasekit-verify/fixtures/mp/fastchebpure.lock), the code accompanying
"Superancillary Equations for the Multiparameter Equations of State in REFPROP
10.0" (I. H. Bell, J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 53, 2024, doi:10.1063/5.0191228).
phasekit changed their layout only: the values are the release's, and p_sa_mp
is p(SA)/p(mp) computed from two of them.

The fastchebpure repository has no licence file (user decision 3a, recorded in
docs/design/04-user-decisions.md, 2026-10-05). As a work of NIST employees it
is generally not subject to copyright protection in the United States
(17 U.S.C. 105); this reading is an inference, not legal advice. phasekit
redistributes the data with credit to NIST and fastchebpure and with the NIST
disclaimer below, which accompanies NIST's software, including the
superancillary code CoolProp ships.
